# NRI portal checks

Use with the base portal screenshot loop. Labels can change by AY; verify the
current ITR-2 instructions and schema.

## Part A – General

Check:

- residential status selected matches the documented day-count result,
- condition/basis for residential status is the matching portal option,
- current-year and preceding-year stay fields are completed when requested,
- jurisdiction of residence and foreign TIN are accurate,
- missing TIN reason is used only when genuinely applicable,
- benefits available only to residents are not selected,
- Portuguese Civil Code treatment is No unless factually applicable,
- FPI is Yes only with registration evidence,
- Section 115H is selected only after verifying eligibility and an intentional
  choice,
- NRI bank accounts and refund nomination are accurately reported.

Do not copy a prior return's residential status without recomputing the current
year.

## Income and treaty fields

- Report Indian-source dividend, interest, gains, and house-property income in
  their normal schedules first.
- Enter DTAA details only for the portion actually claimed under the treaty.
- Match the income item, country code, treaty article, treaty rate, Indian Act
  section/rate, TRC answer, and supporting evidence.
- Do not turn Indian-source income into foreign-source income because the user
  lives abroad.
- Do not select Schedule FSI/TR/FA mechanically. Determine applicability from
  residential status and the nature of the claim.
- Under current general rules, Schedule FA is associated with ROR disclosure;
  verify the selected AY before omitting or including it for NR/RNOR.

## Preview review additions

In the preview PDF, verify:

- status is NR/RNOR/ROR as intended,
- jurisdiction and TIN are present where required,
- treaty rows reconcile to the domestic income rows,
- TRC answer matches actual possession,
- Schedule FA/FSI/TR inclusion or omission has a documented reason,
- no resident-only deduction, rebate, or disclosure was applied accidentally.

If treaty relief materially affects tax and the evidence is incomplete, stop
before submission and present the domestic-law result alongside the pending
treaty result.
