# Preview PDF and acknowledgement review

## Preview PDF gate

Ask the user to download and attach the complete **preview PDF** after all
schedules are confirmed and before submission. Extract and reconcile:

- AY, form, filing section, regime, and residential status.
- Income by head and gross total income.
- Capital-gain buckets, exemptions, and loss set-off.
- House-property result and Other Sources.
- Chapter VI-A deductions.
- CYLA/BFLA/CFL and special-rate schedules.
- Total income, tax, rebate, surcharge, cess, interest, and fee.
- TDS/TCS/advance/self-assessment tax.
- Amount payable or refund and refund bank nomination.
- Verification identity and material declarations.

Compare every figure with the evidence ledger. Report mismatches as:

`PDF value → expected value → source → tax effect → action`

Do not approve merely because portal validation reports zero errors.

## Pre-submission checkpoint

Give the user a short result:

- `Expected tax payable/refund`
- `Evidence still missing`
- `Decisions requiring user confirmation`
- `Warnings or CA escalation`

If self-assessment tax is payable, ask for the challan after the user pays and
verify that the tax-paid schedule and final payable update. Stop before Submit.

## Acknowledgement review

After the user submits, ask for the acknowledgement/ITR-V. Confirm:

- acknowledgement number exists,
- correct AY and ITR form,
- filing section and submission date,
- total income,
- tax payable or refund,
- verification status/mode and verification deadline if pending.

Explain the outcome plainly: refund claimed, tax fully paid, demand remaining,
or verification pending. An acknowledgement records the filed return; it does
not prove that processing or refund credit has completed.

Advise the user to keep the filed return, acknowledgement, computation, source
documents, and relevant certificates securely. Do not copy their identifiers or
documents into the reusable skill.
