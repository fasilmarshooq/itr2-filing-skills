# Tailored intake and reconciliation

## Use this reference

Read this before requesting documents. Ask for one missing item at a time and
say what it will verify. Do not dump the whole catalogue on the user.

## Profile first

Establish only enough facts to tailor collection:

1. FY/AY and original, belated, revised, or updated filing.
2. Income sources: salary/pension; house property; capital gains; interest;
   dividends; foreign income; other income.
3. Prior-year losses, previous ITR, notices, or outstanding demands.
4. Candidate deductions: tuition, insurance, home loan, donations, NPS, and
   other evidence the user already has.

If the user mentions business, freelance, F&O, or business-like intraday
trading, stop and reassess the ITR form.

## Tailored evidence catalogue

| Trigger | Ask for |
|---|---|
| Every filer | PAN/Aadhaar status, relevant bank accounts and IFSC, prior filed ITR/143(1) if useful |
| Department reconciliation | AIS, TIS, and Form 26AS for the same FY |
| Salary/pension | Every Form 16/16A and necessary annexures |
| Shares, mutual funds, ETF, bonds | Tax P&L or realised-gain statement from **every broker/demat**; CAMS/KFintech statement where relevant; transaction detail when summaries cannot support classification |
| Interest/dividend | Bank interest certificates/statements and dividend evidence; reconcile with AIS/26AS |
| House property | Purchase/registration document, possession/completion fact, home-loan interest certificate, principal paid, municipal tax/rent evidence if relevant |
| Children studying in India | School receipt/challan identifying the **tuition** component and payment date; do not claim admission, transport, food, development, or other non-tuition components as tuition |
| Health insurance | 80D premium certificate/receipt, insured members, ages, payment mode, and payer |
| Other 80C | Eligible payment evidence and identifier; apply the combined statutory cap only after verifying the AY rule |
| Exemption against capital gains | Sale/acquisition evidence, dates, costs, transfer expenses, new-house deed/registration/payment evidence, and CGAS evidence if applicable |
| Tax credits | TDS/TCS entries and advance/self-assessment-tax challans |

Do not request portal credentials, passwords, OTPs, or unrelated family
financial records.

## Reconciliation gate

Create one ledger row per material item:

| Source | Fact/amount | Classification | Schedule | Cross-check | Status |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Broker Tax P&L | sale/cost/charge | provisional asset bucket | CG/112A | AIS | Verified/Conflict/Missing |

Before portal entry:

- Match TDS/TCS and tax payments to 26AS.
- Explain AIS sales rather than treating gross proceeds as gains.
- Reconcile each broker/demat separately, then aggregate only compatible asset
  buckets.
- Reconcile interest, dividend, refund interest, and house-property figures.
- Import brought-forward losses from the filed ITR/143(1), not memory.
- Record unresolved differences and their possible tax effect.

A prefilled number and a third-party computation are both claims to verify. Use
source evidence and current law to resolve the difference; do not prefer either
blindly.
